Preventative Horology

Watch-Care Advice

A fine mechanical watch comprises hundreds of miniaturized components interacting under constant dynamic tension. Thoughtful care preserves chronometric precision between overhaul cycles.

  • check_circle Manual Winding: Wind slowly until tension increases gently; never force past resistance.
  • check_circle Magnetic Shielding: Keep watches 10+ cm away from laptop speaker magnets and phone cases.
  • check_circle Crown Position: Always verify the winding crown is fully pressed or screwed in before moisture contact.
Luster & Seal Protection

Cleaning & Daily Maintenance

Routine hygiene preserves the brushed and polished bevels while preventing salt or perspiration crystallization in bracelet articulation pins.

Freshwater Rinse

After saltwater or chlorine exposure, gently rinse water-resistant references (crown firmly closed) under lukewarm tap water and dry with a soft cloth.

Microfiber Crystal Polish

Remove smudges and skin oils from anti-reflective sapphire crystals using the supplied chamois microfiber cloth. Never apply chemical solvents.
