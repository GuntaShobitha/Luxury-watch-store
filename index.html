Atelier Philosophy

The Art of Watchmaking

A chronicle of micromechanical engineering and ancestral discipline: from parametric 3D calculations to final chronometric certification.

01

Design & Micro-Architecture

Parametric calculations establish equilibrium across the openworked caliber, balancing structural component stress with optical transparency.

02

5-Axis CNC Micro-Milling

Robotic cutters mill mainplates and bridges from raw Grade 5 titanium billets within microscopic tolerances of 2 microns.

03

Hand-Anglage & Black Polish

Artisans bevel interior angles using diamond micro-files, followed by zinc plate black-polishing that reflects light with absolute specular brilliance.

04

Single-Watchmaker Assembly

A single dedicated master watchmaker oversees the entire assembly of all 248 components, jewel pressings, and tourbillon cage balancing.

05

500-Hour Chronometric Testing

Each completed watch endures 500 hours of temperature, shock, and 6-position chronometer regulation before receiving its atelier seal.
