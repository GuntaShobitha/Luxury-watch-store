CHRONOS TYPE-0

Skeletonized Tourbillon Flagship Timepiece

Caliber Movement Caliber ST-01 Flying Tourbillon (Manual)

Dimensions 41 mm Diameter × 10.8 mm Thickness
Dial Color & Crystal Obsidian Black Openworked Dial, Sapphire Crystal
